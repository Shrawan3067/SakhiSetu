## Brand & Style

The design system embodies an editorial, mission-driven aesthetic tailored for an ultra-premium social-impact and women’s welfare platform. It balances the institutional credibility of philanthropic governance with the empathetic warmth of grassroots community impact.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Editorial Warmth & Restraint:** High typographic refinement, airy compositions, and grounded earthen tones replace sterile corporate software tropes.
- **Human-Centric Transparency:** Clean metric scorecards, contextual storytelling modules, and dignified human portraits celebrate progress without sensationalism.
- **Tactile Soft Modernism:** Subtle surface differentiation, fine border delineations, and gentle organic radii evoke handcrafted paper goods combined with high-tier digital clarity.

## Colors

The palette establishes grounded trust through botanic greens and warm earthen neutrals, steering clear of clinical blues or high-tech neon tones.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Forest Green (`#174C3C`) & Deep Evergreen (`#103A30`):** Anchors foundational actions, main navigation structures, and high-impact emphasis. Evokes stability, long-term stewardship, and dignified authority.
- **Warm Accent Terracotta (`#C77B59`):** Conveys human vitality, creative initiative, and communal warmth. Used selectively for high-visibility highlights, active participation triggers, and secondary calls-to-action.
- **Accent Muted Sage (`#A8BDA8`):** Offers a calming intermediary layer for subtle pill tags, progress meters, and delicate state backdrops.
- **Canvas & Surface System:** 
  - Canvas Base: Warm Ivory (`#FAF9F5`) provides a soft, unbleached literary tone that prevents optical fatigue.
  - Interactive Surfaces: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) surfaces rise above the ivory foundation to define interactive workspaces and card containers.
- **Typography & Structural Contrast:**
  - Charcoal Primary (`#202923`): Ensures high readability without the harshness of pure black.
  - Muted Charcoal Secondary (`#727B74`): Subtle editorial metadata, dates, and supportive descriptions.
  - Border Linen (`#E8EAE5`): Crisp, low-contrast delineations designed for fine structural borders.
- **Functional Semantics:**
  - Success Emerald (`#267A52`): Verification, micro-grant completion, impact validation.
  - Warning Amber (`#B77932`): Pending identity verification, required action items.
  - Error Crimson (`#C45151`): Validation alerts, system issues, blocked workflows.

## Typography

Typography balances geometric structure and functional readability. 

- **Display & Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Tuned for approachable authority. Tighter letter tracking gives large titles a confident editorial posture.
- **Body & Operational Copy (Inter):** Optimized for high-density legibility, statistical tables, documentation, and long-form impact field stories.
- **Micro-Labels & Meta Indicators:** Use `label-caps` styled in all-caps with generous tracking (`0.08em`) to demarcate categories, governance levels, and financial states cleanly.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an intentional editorial cadence grounded on a modular 8px base rhythm.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column responsive grid with a maximum content canvas of 1280px. 24px gutters provide structural separation, framed by 48px outer gutters for breathing room.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with 20px gutters and 32px canvas margins. Multi-column metric cards stack into paired layouts.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with 16px gutters and 20px outer margins. Content priority flows vertically, with quick-action trays anchored to the viewport bottom.

### Vertical Rhythm
- Maintain a clear typographic scale: Section headings pair with body content using `space-sm` (8px), card internals separate content tiers using `space-md` (16px) to `space-lg` (24px), while distinct thematic chapters split via `space-xl` (40px) or double increments (80px).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy avoids generic blurred drop-shadows. Depth is achieved through refined tonal layering paired with crisp 1px borders.

### Depth Mechanics
- **Ground Floor (Base Canvas):** Warm Ivory (`#FAF9F5`) serves as the physical substrate.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Module Surfaces):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) framed with a 1px border of `#E8EAE5`. Shadows are omitted or limited to an ultra-subtle ambient layer: `0 1px 3px rgba(32, 41, 35, 0.04)`.
- **Layer 2 (Floating Modals & Flyouts):** Pure White with a dual-tiered ambient shadow tinted by Charcoal: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(16, 58, 48, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(32, 41, 35, 0.03)`, bordered by `#E8EAE5`.
- **Interactive Depth on Hover:** Cards subtly elevate without floating abruptly, transitioning the border color to `#A8BDA8` and introducing a calm lift: `0 6px 16px -2px rgba(23, 76, 60, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The geometric signature uses a refined 12px to 16px radius across functional panels, paired with full pill radii for metadata and badges.

### Implementation Guide
- **Containers & Cards:** Primary interactive cards and dashboard containers use `rounded-lg` (16px), creating a friendly yet structured silhouette.
- **Interactive Inputs & Buttons:** Secondary buttons, text inputs, and select triggers standardize on `rounded` (8px to 12px) to preserve compact ergonomic tap targets.
- **Pills & Status Tags:** Micro-badges, category tags, and avatars use fully rounded caps (9999px pill geometry) to contrast against rectilinear card structures.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Background `#174C3C`, foreground `#FFFFFF`, border none, corner radius 12px. Hover: `#103A30`. Focus: 2px ring in Terracotta (`#C77B59`) offset by 2px `#FAF9F5`.
- **Secondary Human Action:** Background `#C77B59`, foreground `#FFFFFF`, corner radius 12px. Used for high-empathy actions like "Pledge Support" or "Join Circle". Hover: `#B56C4A`.
- **Subtle / Outline:** Background transparent, border 1px solid `#E8EAE5`, foreground `#202923`. Hover: Background `#FAF9F5` with border `#A8BDA8`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills:** Background `#FAF9F5`, border 1px solid `#E8EAE5`, text `#202923`, font `label-sm`.
- **Verified Impact Tag:** Background tinted Sage (`rgba(168, 189, 168, 0.2)`), text `#103A30`, border 1px solid `rgba(23, 76, 60, 0.2)`, fully rounded with an emerald dot indicator (`#267A52`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E8EAE5`, padding 12px 16px, text `#202923`, font `body-md`, radius 10px. Active focus transitions border to `#174C3C` with an ambient glow of `rgba(23, 76, 60, 0.12)`.
- **Selection Controls:** Custom checkboxes and radios feature a 2px border in `#727B74` that switches to a solid `#174C3C` fill with a white checkmark when selected.

### Impact Cards
- **Structure:** Pure White container, 16px corner radius, 1px solid `#E8EAE5`, internal padding 24px.
- **Composition:** Features a top row with `label-caps` categorization and pill badges, followed by a `headline-sm` title, a concise field summary, an inline funding or outcome progress bar (`#174C3C` on `#E8EAE5` rail), and a bottom-aligned metadata footer in `#727B74`.

### Transparency & Trust Metric Modules
- Minimalist data units featuring oversized numerical values rendered in `Plus Jakarta Sans` bold, accented with a 3px vertical accent bar in Terracotta (`#C77B59`) or Forest Green (`#174C3C`), establishing verifiable operational clarity.
