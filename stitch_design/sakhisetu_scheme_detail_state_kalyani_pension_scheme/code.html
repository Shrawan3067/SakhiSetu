Detailed Scheme Overview & Mandate

Enacted under the administrative jurisdiction of the Madhya Pradesh Social Security Rules, the Mukhyamantri Kalyani Sahayata Yojana serves as a targeted social safety net for women who have lost their spouse. By replacing older, discretionary widow grant mechanisms with unified direct-benefit transfers, the state ensures uninterrupted monthly subsistence.

shield Core Protection Target

Protects widowed women across rural and semi-urban gram panchayats from immediate poverty traps, arbitrary familial dispossession, and medical deprivation following the demise of the primary household earner.

sync Transition at Age 80

Upon attaining the age of 80 years, the beneficiary is automatically migrated to the Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS), enhancing the monthly entitlement from ₹600 to ₹1,000 per month without re-application.

favorite

Kalyani Remarriage Incentive Grant (कल्याणी पुनर्विवाह योजना)

Should an active pensioner decide to remarry, monthly pension ceases, but she becomes statutorily eligible for a one-time fixed rehabilitation grant of ₹2,00,000 (Two Lakh Rupees) under the state remarriage welfare package.

Workflow Guidance

Step-by-Step Application Process

Applications are completely digitized via the Samagra Social Security portal. Follow this timeline to ensure submission without administrative bottlenecks:

1

Samagra Biometric eKYC Validation

Visit any Common Service Center (CSC), MP Online Kiosk, or Gram Panchayat Kendra. Authenticate biometric fingerprints or OTP to verify Samagra-Aadhaar linking.

Average Time: 15 Mins
2

Dossier Submission at Panchayat / Ward Office

Submit the signed Form 3 with supporting documents to your Gram Panchayat Sachiv (Rural) or Ward Office/Zone Commissioner (Urban). Obtain an official physical receipt acknowledgement.

Acknowledgment Generated: Day 1
3

Scrutiny & Janpad CEO Verification

Janpad Panchayat CEO or Urban Project Officer conducts statutory field verification and cross-verifies widow records against the Civil Registry within statutory timelines.

Mandatory Window: 15 Working Days
4

Digital Sanction & First Disbursal

Upon approval, the PPO (Pension Payment Order) is digitally issued on the portal. Monthly DBT of ₹600 commences on the 1st week of the subsequent calendar month directly into your bank.

Proactive Guidance

Why Applications Get Rejected & Solutions

cancel Missing NPCI Mapping

Aadhaar is linked to the bank account, but NPCI Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) route is not turned on.

Solution: Submit Mandate Form at bank branch for DBT activation.
cancel Name Discrepancies

Spelling mismatches between Husband’s Death Certificate and the applicant’s Samagra Member profile.

Solution: Request Samagra profile correction at CSC before applying.
cancel Unseeded Samagra ID

The applicant appears on two different family ration or Samagra cards, causing automatic portal freeze.

Solution: Delete old entry via Gram Panchayat Sachiv dashboard.
